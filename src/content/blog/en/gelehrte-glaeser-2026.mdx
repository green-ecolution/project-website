"Science for everyone" is the motto of the Gelehrte Gläser, which translates roughly to "scholarly glasses": instead of inviting people into a lecture hall, researchers go where people like to sit anyway, into the cafés and bars of Flensburg's city center. Anyone who feels like it can listen in over a drink and then talk to the researchers directly. Behind the format stand the CIVU, the Europa-Universität Flensburg, the Flensburg University of Applied Sciences and the Duale Hochschule Schleswig-Holstein. On 30 June 2026 the talks spread across the city center between 3 and 8 pm, two per venue.

## Smart irrigation at Lille Lykke

Green Ecolution was a guest at Lille Lykke on Norderstraße at 4:30 pm. Together with Simon Olberding, under the title "Green Ecolution: Smart irrigation of urban green spaces", I showed what the project is about: soil moisture sensors capture what happens below the surface, and the readings turn into irrigation plans and routes for everyday operations. The subject matter lies literally around the corner: the [test sensors](/blog/acht-von-zehn-sensoren) already sit in Flensburg's soil.

For a project at home in public space there is hardly a more fitting stage: the trees it is about stand just a few streets away. Formats like the Gelehrte Gläser create that closeness, so a warm thank you goes to the organizers.
