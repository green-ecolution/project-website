"Think openly – act openly – connect openly": under this motto, the Smart City Amt Süderbrarup hosted the Smart Region Day on 4 February 2026. Between 2:00 and 5:30 pm the day explored how open source, open learning formats and open cooperation advance digitalization in rural areas, with contributions from politics, business and municipalities, project presentations and room for networking. For Green Ecolution this is home turf: the platform is open source, grows out of close collaboration with a municipality and is built so that others can adopt it.

## What we showed

We took part with a project presentation and told the Green Ecolution story from start to finish. It begins with the hardware: soil moisture sensors that transmit their readings energy-efficiently over LoRaWAN. On top of that comes the application, which is meant to show from the data which trees and green spaces actually need water.

![The hardware side of the project: a soil moisture sensor with its LoRaWAN radio unit](../../../assets/blog/smart-region-day-2026-sensorik.jpg)

The closing part was the operations planning, the piece of the platform that is meant to turn readings into concrete work: irrigation, tree care, maintenance and route planning in one system, with optimized routes for less effort in daily operations.

![Operations planning in the application: irrigation, tree care, maintenance and route planning in one system](../../../assets/blog/smart-region-day-2026-einsatzplanung.jpg)

## Connecting openly, taken literally

The rest of the afternoon belonged to what the format is made for: exchange with municipalities, companies and initiatives that want to shape the digital transformation of rural areas. That is exactly the environment Green Ecolution is meant to work in, which made Süderbrarup the right place for us to present the project. Thanks to the Smart City Amt Süderbrarup for the invitation and the organization.
