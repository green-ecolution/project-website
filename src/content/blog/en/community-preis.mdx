Green Ecolution takes part in this year's Open Source Wettbewerb. The competition addresses public administrations at the federal, state and municipal level and awards pioneering projects that advance the digital transformation with open source. The application was submitted by the Digitalagentur Smarte Grenzregion GmbH. Alongside the jury categories there is one award decided by the public: the community award, and that is exactly the one we are running for.

## What we are running with

The core of the application fits into three words: sensors over gut feeling. Green Ecolution shows which urban trees and green spaces really need water, and automatically creates irrigation plans and optimized routes from current sensor data. The solution is open source, developed from practice and open to every municipality. If you follow this blog, you already know part of that practice up close: the [test sensors](/blog/acht-von-zehn-sensoren) are already in the ground across Flensburg.

The application sheet accordingly lists everyone behind the project: the Flensburg University of Applied Sciences, the Technisches Betriebszentrum Flensburg, PROGEEK, the Smarte Grenzregion and the City of Flensburg.

![The application for the community award of the Open Source Wettbewerb](../../../assets/blog/community-preis-bewerbung.png)

## Vote until 30 September

The community vote runs from 17 August to 30 September 2026. During that period you can cast your vote directly on the competition's website: [open-source-wettbewerb.de/voting/green-ecolution](https://open-source-wettbewerb.de/voting/green-ecolution/). The awards will be presented in mid-October at the Smart Country Convention.

Every vote helps to give the project and the topic of data-driven irrigation more visibility. Thank you for your support.
