Green Ecolution takes part in this year's Open Source Wettbewerb, a German competition for open source in the public administration. The application was submitted by the Digitalagentur Smarte Grenzregion GmbH, and it entered the one category where you get a say: we are running for the community award, which is decided by a public vote.

## What we are running with

The core of the application fits into three words: sensors over gut feeling. Green Ecolution shows which urban trees and green spaces really need water, and automatically creates irrigation plans and optimized routes from current sensor data. The solution is open source, developed from practice and open to every municipality. If you follow this blog, you already know part of that practice up close: [eight of ten test sensors](/blog/acht-von-zehn-sensoren) are already in the ground across Flensburg.

Accordingly, the application carries everyone behind the project: the Flensburg University of Applied Sciences, the Technisches Betriebszentrum Flensburg, PROGEEK, the Smarte Grenzregion and the City of Flensburg.

![The application for the community award of the Open Source Wettbewerb](../../../assets/blog/community-preis-bewerbung.png)

## Vote until 30 September

The community vote runs until 30 September 2026. You can cast your vote directly on the competition's website: [open-source-wettbewerb.de/voting/green-ecolution](https://open-source-wettbewerb.de/voting/green-ecolution/).

Every vote helps to give the project and the topic of data-driven irrigation more visibility. Thank you for your support.
